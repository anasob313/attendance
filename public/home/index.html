Attendance

A free personal app for keeping track of your own working hours.

Open Attendance

What the app does

  • Clock in and clock out with one tap, with a live timer for the working day.
  • One reminder notification before you complete your working hours (20 minutes before, by default).
  • Record remote work days and leave (annual, sick and public holidays).
  • Calendar and monthly reports, with export to an Excel file.
  • Works offline and syncs when you are back online.
  • Works best on iPhone when added to the Home Screen.

Each person has a private account. Your records are visible only to you.

Signing in with Google

You can create your account with Continue with Google. Attendance asks Google only for your name and email address (the basic openid, email and profile permissions). They are used only to create your account and sign you in. Attendance does not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, files or any other Google data, and never posts anything on your behalf.

How your data is handled is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Cost

Attendance is free, non-commercial and contains no advertising.
