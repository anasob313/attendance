Attendance

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how Attendance (the web app at https://anasob313.github.io/attendance/, "the app", "we") handles your information. Attendance is a free, non-commercial personal attendance tracker. We collect only what the app needs to work, we do not sell or share your data for advertising, and you can delete your account and all of your records at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • If you sign in with Google: Google shares your name, email address, Google account identifier and profile picture link (the basic openid, email and profile permissions). The sign-in service stores these with your account. The app uses only your name (shown in your account) and email address (to identify your account). We do not request access to Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Drive or any other Google service.
  • If you have an email-and-password account: your email address and your password, which is stored only as a secure one-way hash.

Attendance information you enter

  • Clock-in and clock-out times and the work date.
  • Remote work days.
  • Leave: type (annual, sick, public holiday), start and end dates, and an optional note.
  • Settings: daily working hours, reminder lead time, working days and time zone.

Notification information (only if you turn on reminders)

  • A push subscription created by your browser: a delivery address at your device's push service and the encryption keys needed to send to it.
  • A short device label (for example "iPhone"), so you can tell your devices apart.
  • The scheduled reminder time for an open working day and whether it was delivered.

Technical information

Our hosting and database providers automatically process technical data such as IP address, browser type and request times in their server logs, for security and to operate the service. The app itself uses no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies and no third-party trackers.

Information stored on your device

To work offline, the app keeps your sign-in session, a copy of your own records and any changes waiting to sync in your browser's local storage. Signing out removes the stored copy of your records from that device.

2. How we use information

Only to provide the app's features:

  • creating your account and signing you in;
  • saving your attendance records and calculating hours, statuses and monthly reports;
  • exporting your records to a file when you ask;
  • sending the one working-hours reminder you enabled;
  • keeping the service secure and fixing problems.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, build profiles, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

3. Google user data

Attendance's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The name and email address received from Google are used only to create and identify your Attendance account. They are not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not read by people except when you ask for help, when needed for security, or when required by law.

You can remove Attendance's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. This stops Google sign-in but does not delete your Attendance records; to delete them, use Delete account in the app (section 6).

5. Privacy between users and security

  • Every account is private. Database row-level security ensures a signed-in person can read and change only their own records.
  • All connections use HTTPS encryption. Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • The app's administrator has technical access to the database for maintenance. Your records are not viewed except to fix a problem you report, for security, or when required by law.
  • No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use industry-standard protections and keep access to a minimum.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Your information is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Export: in the app, open Account → Export all records to download your data.
  • Delete: Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all of your attendance records, leave, settings, reminders and notification subscriptions immediately. This cannot be undone.
  • Turning off notifications on a device removes that device's push subscription.
  • Technical logs kept by our providers are deleted on their own standard schedules.

If you cannot sign in and want your data deleted, or want a copy of it, contact us (section 9) from the email address of your account.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your information yourself in the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under data-protection law; contact us to use them.

8. Children

Attendance is intended for working adults and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: attendance-app-support@googlegroups.com

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.
